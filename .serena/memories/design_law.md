# Human-grade design law (WAJIB untuk semua UI)

Body ≥16px; line-height 1.5–1.65 body / 1.15–1.25 judul; ≤50–75 char/baris; jangan center >2 baris;
kontras WCAG AA ≥4.5:1 (jangan `text-gray-400` di atas putih); grid 8px; focus ring `focus-visible:ring-2`.

**HINDARI (anti-pattern "AI aesthetic"):** gradien neon ungu/cyan, blob/sphere 3D glassmorphic, `backdrop-blur-3xl`,
"centered buzzword soup", kartu raksasa kosong, bento tanpa makna, `rounded-3xl` semua container, drop-shadow tiap card, state loading/edge hilang.

Skill `human-grade-web-design` terpasang di Hermes, opencode, dan agy.
